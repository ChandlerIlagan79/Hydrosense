Welcome to HydroSense

Smart Hydration Monitoring Technology

Real-Time Analysis

HydroSense is an innovative hydration assessment platform designed to help individuals better understand their body's hydration status. By analyzing a urine sample through an Arduino-based sensing system, HydroSense translates physical data into meaningful hydration insights that are securely transmitted and displayed on this website.

Advanced Technology

Our system leverages sensor technology and data processing to evaluate hydration indicators from the sample, providing users with an accessible, technology-driven approach to hydration monitoring. HydroSense aims to support awareness, prevention, and smarter daily health decisions through simple, non-invasive analysis.

Seamless Integration

Explore how HydroSense connects hardware and digital intelligence to transform hydration monitoring into a practical and user-friendly experience. Our platform bridges the gap between physical sensors and actionable health insights.
